Founded in 1999, Arkane® Studios has grown to become one of the most renowned and critically acclaimed development teams in the video game industry. Arkane has earned a reputation for creating games with unique style, immersive action, and a hand-crafted approach. The studio’s titles, which include Dishonored®, Dishonored 2, Prey®, and DEATHLOOP™ have won hundreds of prestigious accolades around the globe, including several "Game of the Year" awards.
